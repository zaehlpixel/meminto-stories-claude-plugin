# Choose the degree and purpose of editing

Read this when the user asks for a particular style or help choosing an
editing approach. Use their requested approach directly; do not force a menu
or require a named mode. Hemingway-inspired clarity is an option, not an
imitation of an author's complete literary voice or a universal quality rule.

| Approach | Useful changes | Preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gentle polish | Repair awkward phrasing and unintended repetition with minimal intervention. | Personal expressions, vocabulary, rhythm and narrator. |
| Clear and concise | Untangle difficult sentences, remove genuinely redundant phrasing, prefer concrete words and suitable verbs. | Meaning, uncertainty, emphasis and useful longer sentences. |
| More vivid | Foreground concrete observations already in the source; suggest a focused recollection outside the draft if detail is missing. | Factual boundaries; never invent weather, smells, dialogue, feelings or actions. |
| Spoken voice | Make dictation readable with paragraphs and punctuation; remove only distracting speech disfluencies. | Dialect, memorable turns of phrase and the speaker's individual voice. |
| Reader orientation | Clarify supported pronoun references and explain terms using supplied context; ask about genuinely missing context. | Intended audience, privacy boundaries and the author's viewpoint. |
| Rhythm and transitions | Vary sentence shape and connect supplied ideas where the relation is known. | Deliberate repetition, pacing and factual sequence; do not invent causation. |

Treat passives, adverbs, nominalizations, long sentences and repetitions as
possible places to inspect, not automatic errors. A stronger verb must not
change the event: walking cannot silently become running. Removing "perhaps",
"I think" or "as far as I remember" may turn uncertainty into a false fact.
Retain it unless the author supplies a correction. Do not flatten a distinctive
voice into generic polished prose or add a sentimental moral at the end.

Adapt to the actual language. Do not apply English school-grade thresholds or
invent numerical readability scores for German or another language. An
explanation with concrete examples is often more useful than a score.
Preserve paragraph breaks and the supported media layout. A style choice does
not authorize changing headings, captions, contributors or the section order.

For example, a fictional demonstration of factual editing:

Original: "Ich glaube, dass es im Sommer 1987 gewesen sein muss, als wir dann
nach der langen Fahrt endlich angekommen waren."

Clearer: "Ich glaube, es war im Sommer 1987. Nach der langen Fahrt kamen wir
endlich an."

The uncertainty remains. Show a different intensity if requested, then preview
the exact selected stored revision and obtain approval through the normal
rewrite workflow. Examples in this reference are never facts for a user's book.
